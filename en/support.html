Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my subscriptions stored?

Only on your iPhone. Abonaut needs no account and has no server for your data. If you delete the app, the data is gone. You can back it up with the export (see below).

How do I export my subscriptions?

In Abonaut under Settings → Export as CSV. The file opens in Excel or Numbers and can be saved to iCloud Drive, for example.

How do I add several subscriptions at once?

In Abonaut, tap + → Several from Catalogue and select all the providers you have. Then check the next payment date and add them all together.

How do I find my subscriptions in my bank statement?

Export the last 12 months of transactions from your e-banking or bank app as CSV, camt.053 (XML) or PDF. Credit card statements work too. Then tap + → From Statement in Abonaut and choose the file. Abonaut looks for payments that repeat regularly and shows them for you to choose. The file stays on your iPhone and isn’t saved.

Unzip zipped files first by tapping them in the Files app. Abonaut can’t read scanned PDFs without text; use CSV or camt.053 instead.

I don’t get any reminders.

Check in iOS under Settings → Notifications → Abonaut that notifications are allowed. In Abonaut under Settings → Reminders you can set the lead time, time of day and types of reminders. Open Abonaut now and then so the next reminders get scheduled.

How do I protect Abonaut with Face ID?

Long-press the Abonaut icon on the Home Screen and choose Require Face ID. iOS will then ask for Face ID every time you open it.

How are monthly costs calculated?

Each subscription is projected over a year and divided by twelve, also for weekly or custom intervals. For shared subscriptions, only your share counts. Paused and cancelled subscriptions don’t count, nor do those in a free trial. Amounts in euros or dollars are converted with the exchange rates shown.

Where do the prices in the savings suggestions come from?

From a provider catalogue that we check regularly. Suggestions with catalogue prices only appear if the price was checked within the last six months. All prices without guarantee. If you spot a wrong price, write to us.

How do the AI savings tips work?

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Abonaut creates personal tips for your subscriptions in the Savings tab. Everything happens on your iPhone, your subscriptions never leave the device. Abonaut checks every tip against your data. Tips are without guarantee.

What is Abonaut Plus?

An optional subscription that supports Abonaut: no ads, widgets for the Home and Lock Screen, custom categories and insights (costs for the next twelve months, most expensive subscriptions). Abonaut Plus is available monthly or yearly. Everything else stays free.

How do I cancel Abonaut Plus?

In Abonaut under Settings → Manage Subscription, or in iOS under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Abonaut Plus stays active until the end of the paid period. Custom categories are kept.

I bought Abonaut Plus, but it isn’t active.

In Abonaut, open the Abonaut Plus page and tap Restore Subscription. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account as when you bought it. If that doesn’t help, write to us.

How do I change my ad consent?

In Abonaut under Settings → Change Ad Consent. You manage tracking in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More in the privacy policy.
