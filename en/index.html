Subsy

Track your subscriptions, keep an eye on costs and discover ways to save. An app for iPhone, made in Switzerland.

  • Costs per month and per year, also for shared subscriptions and in euros or dollars
  • Reminders before payments, trial endings and cancellation deadlines
  • Suggestions for cheaper plans and duplicate subscriptions
  • No account needed, your subscriptions stay on your iPhone
