Privacy Policy

As of 17 November 2026. In case of discrepancies, the German version prevails.

In short: Abonaut needs no account. Your subscriptions, settings and reminders stay on your iPhone. We have no access to them. Abonaut is funded by ads from Google. We ask for your permission before showing personalised ads. With Abonaut Plus you see no ads. Payment goes through Apple, we receive no payment data.

This policy applies to the iPhone app Abonaut. It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Your subscriptions stay on your device

Everything you enter in Abonaut (subscriptions, prices, notes, payment method as free text, settings) is stored only on your iPhone. No Abonaut server receives this data. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Abonaut’s widgets show a summary (monthly costs, upcoming payments). For this, the app stores a copy of this information in a protected area of your iPhone that only Abonaut and its widgets can read.

If you import subscriptions from a bank statement, Abonaut reads the file only on your iPhone to find payments that repeat regularly. The file and the individual transactions are neither saved nor transmitted. Only the subscriptions you select are added.

The AI savings tips are created by Apple Intelligence directly on your iPhone. Your subscriptions are not sent to us, to Apple or to any third party. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, these tips are not available.

Once Abonaut offers iCloud sync, your data will also be stored in your private iCloud, operated by Apple. Only you and your devices have access, not us.

3. Reminders

The app schedules reminders for payments, trials and cancellation deadlines directly on your iPhone. No data is sent to us or to third parties.

4. Provider catalogue and exchange rates

For savings suggestions, the app regularly downloads a price list from our website, hosted by GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA). For technical reasons, GitHub receives your IP address and device information, as with any website visit. Your subscriptions are not transmitted. Matching your subscriptions against the price list happens only on your device.

In the same way, the app downloads a package with the logos of all providers, always the full package. So nobody can tell which subscriptions you have. You can turn logos off in Settings; the package is then no longer downloaded.

Partner links in the “Discover” tab

Some links to providers are partner links and are marked as advertising. If you open such a link, the provider or its partner programme learns that you came from Abonaut, and Abonaut may receive a commission if you subscribe. Abonaut shares no details about your subscriptions. What happens on the provider’s website is governed by its own privacy policy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Abonaut shows banner ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA. Ads appear only in the overview and in the savings suggestions, never when adding a subscription.

To show and bill ads, Google processes, among other things:

  • a device identifier (Apple’s advertising identifier only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it (for example the country),
  • information about the ads shown and whether you tap them,
  • technical diagnostic data, for example about loading times.

Your subscriptions and all other content of the app are not used for advertising and not sent to Google.

Your consent

On first launch, Abonaut asks with a Google form whether you agree to personalised ads. Then iOS asks whether Abonaut may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, Google still shows ads, but they are not tailored to you.

You can change your choice at any time:

  • Ad consent: in Abonaut under Settings → Change Ad Consent.
  • Tracking: in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The legal basis for personalised ads is your consent; for showing ads in general, our legitimate interest in funding the free app. More on how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Abonaut Plus (in-app subscription)

Abonaut Plus is a subscription you take out in the app via Apple. Purchase, payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland), under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We receive no payment data and no information about you. The app only asks Apple whether a valid Abonaut Plus exists on your device and remembers the result locally. Apple gives us aggregated sales figures without reference to individuals.

No ads with Abonaut Plus means: the app no longer loads ads from Google.

7. Transfers abroad

Google, GitHub and Apple may also process data in the USA. Transfers rely on recognised safeguards, such as certification under the Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

8. Export

If you export your subscriptions as a CSV file, the file is created on your iPhone. You decide where to send or store it afterwards.

9. No analytics tools

Abonaut contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own. If you have allowed sharing analytics data with developers in iOS, Apple provides us with anonymised crash reports.

10. Your rights

You can request information about your data, have it corrected or deleted and withdraw consent at any time. Since we don’t store any data about you ourselves, contact Google for data held by Google. For questions, reach us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or a supervisory authority in the EU.

11. Changes

If the app changes, we update this policy. The version published here applies.
